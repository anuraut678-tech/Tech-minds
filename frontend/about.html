ABOUT SIF SAFETY

Building a Safer Workplace

SIF Safety is a workplace safety platform designed to help organizations identify hazards, report near-misses, analyze risks and prevent serious incidents using AI-powered safety analysis.

What is SIF Safety?

SIF Safety stands for Serious Injury and Fatality prevention. Our platform focuses on identifying workplace conditions that could potentially lead to serious injuries or fatal incidents.

Employees can report near-misses and unsafe conditions through a simple digital platform. The system can then use AI analysis to identify important safety information.

Our goal is simple: identify risks early, understand why they happen and take preventive action before an incident occurs.
