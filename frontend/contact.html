Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about SIF Safety.

What is a near-miss?

A near-miss is an unsafe event that could have resulted in an injury or serious incident but did not.

Why should I report a near-miss?

Reporting helps identify hazards before they cause serious injuries or fatalities.

How does Gemini AI help?

Gemini AI can assist in analyzing reports, identifying possible risks and suggesting preventive actions.

Is my report important?

Yes. Every report provides valuable information that can help improve workplace safety.
